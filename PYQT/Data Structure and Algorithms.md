3) Learn Time and Space Complexit

To ace programming interviews, you need to know how fast and how much space your choice takes.

That means you have to learn how to measure the performance of data structures and algorithms, and how to compare different solutions based on their efficiency.

Learn the Big O notation.

4) Practice implementating the data structures and algorithms

Don't just rely on existing libraries for data structures and algorithms.
Try to code them from scratch to learn their inner workings and show your skills in a new setting.
